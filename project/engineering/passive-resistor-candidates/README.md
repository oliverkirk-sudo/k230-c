2026-09-30. Audit of the high-temperature CM-K230 candidate, with 38 × 38 mm, top-only components, 140 edge pins, 1 GB LPDDR4 and 16 GB eMMC retained. The provisional 85°C enclosed-air target is **not** a verified resistor/PCB temperature. The initial source audit made no CAD/population/purchase changes. Subsequently authorized HT-DRAFT13 implements only U14 as a Schmitt OR and R33=270k; the classifications below retain the explicitly frozen pre-change input. See the current [implementation validation](../../cad/high-temp-candidate/u14-or-validation.json) for that adopted candidate.

The frozen input contains 61 resistor references: 19 already assigned precision parts and **42 unassigned positions**. The latter divide into **35 ordinary nonzero resistors, two precision startup-selection resistors and five role-specific zero links**. Three of the 42 are DNP. [Calculations](static-screen.json) are reproducible with `python engineering/passive-resistor-candidates/calculate_screen.py`; they use the frozen [input extract](input-snapshot.json) and the project's static regulator bounds.

**U14 follow-on gate:** the43 kΩ R33 proposal below is a static-load correction only. [The input-slew addendum](U14_SLEW_ADDENDUM.md) finds an unclosed10 ns/V LVC input requirement and evaluates the already-reviewed AUP97 as a correctly wired Schmitt OR, with a separate270 kΩ output-pullup proposal. ## Family, temperature and land geometry

Vishay **CRCW0201 e3**, document 20052, is a credible *layout candidate* for these ordinary values. Specify **F tolerance / K TCR**: ±1% initial and ±100 ppm/K, supported over **47 Ω–1 MΩ**. The ±200 ppm/K ±1% range is 10 Ω–10 MΩ; 1–9.76 Ω has asymmetric −200/+400 ppm/K. Do not accidentally substitute the N-code 200 ppm grade for the K-code candidate. Schema-derived ED candidates used here are CRCW02014K70FKED, CRCW020110K0FKED, CRCW020122K0FKED, CRCW020143K0FKED, CRCW020147K0FKED, CRCW0201100KFKED and CRCW02011M00FKED. Stock and lifecycle were not verified. [Vishay pp1–2](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20052/crcw0201e3.pdf)

The family specifies −55…155°C operation, 30 V limiting element voltage and 50 mW at 70°C. Its graph decreases linearly to zero at 155°C: **41.18 mW at 85°C, 29.41 mW at 105°C and 17.65 mW at 125°C**. These graph readings require the manufacturer's PCB/thermal conditions and film ≤155°C; 85°C enclosure air does not prove them. Working voltage also remains limited by √(P·R). The worst proposed ordinary full-rail DC illustration is R204 at 5.5 V across 9 kΩ: **3.361 mW**. Actual hot spots, PCB heat flow, pulses and source voltage still need review.

The source drawing was visually checked: body **0.60±0.03 × 0.30±0.03 × 0.23±0.03 mm**, two **0.28 × 0.43 mm** copper pads, **0.23 mm inner gap**, pad centers ±0.255 mm, **0.79 × 0.43 mm total copper envelope**. A 1.1 × 0.7 mm planning box provides only 0.155/0.135 mm per-side allowance beyond copper. A hypothetical 0.05 mm mask expansion plus 0.25 mm clearance instead gives **1.39 × 1.03 mm**, rounded outward to 1.4 × 1.1 mm. Neither clearance convention is manufacturer assembly approval; mask, paste, placement and inspection capability remain open. These are source dimensions, not permission to assign a generic 0201 footprint.

## Accuracy is an acceptance requirement, not a lifetime model

This report deliberately tests **±10% total resistance** for ordinary candidates. It is a required acceptance interval spanning initial tolerance, temperature, assembly, aging and environment, **not a guaranteed CRCW life specification**. Vishay permits ±(2%R+0.1 Ω) change in the 70°C/1000 h endurance test and ±(4%R+0.1 Ω) at 8000 h; 155°C/1000 h upper-category endurance permits ±(2%R+0.1 Ω), and soldering heat permits ±(1%R+0.05 Ω). These are different prescribed tests. They cannot be added and relabeled as a guaranteed arbitrary mission lifetime. At 100 ppm/K, a 20→125°C excursion alone corresponds to 1.05% resistance change. Mission duration, humidity, film temperature, mounting shift and process still need an actual acceptance plan. [Vishay pp1,3–4](https://www.vishay.com/docs/20052/crcw0201e3.pdf)

**R205/R206 are excluded from that interval.** TI's startup selection table specifies 56.2 kΩ with ±1% accuracy for 0.8 V/address 0x49. TNPW0402 supports 56.2 kΩ at ±0.1%/10 ppm/K; candidate **TNPW040256K2BYED** can use the separately reviewed source-land footprint. The existing conditional **±0.70% total** acceptance gives 55.8066–56.5934 kΩ, inside TI's 55.638–56.762 kΩ band. It remains a total mission-profile requirement, not an automatic life guarantee. [TI Table8-1 p12](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62864.pdf), [Vishay p3](https://www.vishay.com/docs/28758/tnpw_e3.pdf), [existing footprint review](../../cad/verified-footprints/passive-candidates/README.md)

## Zero links remain role-specific

Keep **R220 WSL060300000ZEA9** and **R561 WFZ040200000ZE66** under their [existing independent power-link reviews](../mechanical/passive-small-parts-review.md). Do not substitute commodity0201.

**R221** may retain CRCW02010000Z0ED as a *conditional* layout option: the Canaan50 mA VDD3P3_SD budget would cause2.5 mV drop and0.125 mW at50 mΩ. This is a SoC supply branch, not the TF card's main feed. The CRCW jumper's1 A rating is explicitly at70°C; hot resistance/current, peak/inrush and branch drop remain unqualified. Do not infer a125°C current rating from a resistor power graph alone.

**R528**, still DNP, is a low-current logic strap: if deliberately fitted, the known100 kΩ pulldown/input path draws at most≈21.151 µA at the stated corners. CRCW02010000Z0ED is a role-specific physical candidate; populating it would still require the separate boot-voltage qualification decision. **R401** remains selection-deferred until actual PMU input/internal-pull current, firmware input ownership and local ramp are checked. A0 Ω connection to its own domain does not permit firmware to drive that pin low.

This audit establishes candidate family compatibility, source land dimensions and bounded calculations. It does not establish full-board fit, full-supply logic, timing, assembly capability, lifetime, thermal performance or production qualification.
