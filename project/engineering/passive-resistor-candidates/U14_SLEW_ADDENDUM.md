# U14 input slew and OR alternative

2026-09-30. **R33=43 kΩ closes the earlier LVC DC-load screen only; it does not close U14's input-slew requirement.** Do not treat that resistor change alone as acceptance of U14. This source review initially made no CAD changes. A subsequent authorized HT-DRAFT13 change implements the AUP97 true-OR/270k candidate reviewed below; [implementation validation](../../cad/high-temp-candidate/u14-or-validation.json) records the exact nets and bounded checks. [Machine-readable source-screen calculations](u14-slew-addendum.json) are regenerated by `calculate_slew_addendum.py`.

## Existing SN74LVC1G32DBVR

TI specifies input Δt/ΔV ≤**10 ns/V** for VCC3.3 V±0.3 V. GLOBAL_DISABLE rises through R32=10 kΩ from an open-drain U93; it also loads two TMUX control inputs and U14.A. At R32max11 kΩ and9.75 µA total sink leakage, the first-order final high is3.137880 V. An illustrative0.8→2.0 V crossing gives Δt=RC·ln[(Vfinal−0.8)/(Vfinal−2.0)]. Even its *average*10 ns/V screen requires **C≤1.515 pF**; applying the limit to local slope at2.0 V gives1.034 pF. U14's4 pF typical input plus two TMUX3 pF typical inputs already totals10 pF, before U93/trace/probe capacitance, and would give approximately**66 ns/V average**. Those capacitances are not guaranteed bounds and this interval is an analytical screen, not a newly asserted TI measurement definition. The result identifies an unclosed, credible slew concern; it is not an observed failure. [TI RevW pp6–7](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g32.pdf), [TMUX RevC p6](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux1574.pdf)

MODE_TF is a **cold-only** strap: open is pulled low through R31; closed connects it to3V3. Keeping JP1 fixed avoids a commanded transition during stable powered operation. That does not qualify input tracking during supply rise, contact settling or startup output state. Powered strap changes remain outside the design contract.

U14.Y drives only **U13.EN**, R33 and parasitics. Its normal rise/fall is driven by the **push-pull output**, not R33·C. TI gives a4 ns propagation-delay ceiling at125°C/3.3 V±0.3 V under its50 pF/500 Ω measurement network with input tr/tf≤2.5 ns. This is not a separate output-rise-time guarantee and cannot be applied directly to the slow GLOBAL_DISABLE input. TI's application guidance supports ≤50 pF total output load; actual pad/trace/probe capacitance has not been established. The TMUX3 pF input figure is typical only. [TI pp8,10,13–14](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g32.pdf)

## Bounded reuse of 74AUP1G97GW,125

**Correct the proposed truth-table wiring:** ordinary OR is **A=1, Y=B OR C**. Tying B high instead implements **A OR NOT C**. For the GW/SOT363-2 package, use pin1 B=GLOBAL_DISABLE, pin2 GND, pin3 A=3V3, pin4 Y=DISABLE_OR_TF, pin5 VCC=3V3 and pin6 C=MODE_TF. B/C can be interchanged for OR. This requires the reviewed six-pin footprint and pin map; it is not a drop-in LVC five-pin substitution. [Nexperia Rev14 Table4 and Figure6, pp3–5](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74AUP1G97.pdf)

The [existing slow-input review](../mechanical/nexperia-aup97-slow-input-review.json) supports these characterized Schmitt inputs at valid stable supply: the datasheet has VT+/VT−/hysteresis specifications and no input-transition restriction, with supporting manufacturer handbook guidance. This is not an invented “unlimited” table entry. At125°C, replacing the5 µA LVC input with0.75 µA AUP input reduces GLOBAL_DISABLE released leakage to**5.5 µA** including U93, and gives high≥**3.184630 V**. MODE_TF's worst default low becomes**52.25 mV**. The discrete3.0 V threshold row gives VT+max2.32 V and VT−min0.88 V: the illustrative high/low margins are0.864630 V/0.49 V for GLOBAL_DISABLE, and0.925130 V/0.82775 V for MODE_TF. **Extending that discrete threshold row to the actual3.245–3.392 V rail remains an explicit signoff item.** Supply and inputs must stay≤3.6 V, leaving only207.836 mV static headroom above the modeled3V3 maximum; overshoot must be checked.

For its output, **43 kΩ is insufficient to use the AUP continuous-range light-load guarantee**:89.653 µA exceeds20 µA. A separate **R33=270 kΩ±10% proposal** (243–297 kΩ; schema candidate CRCW0201270KFKED) gives worst low-state load**15.9595 µA**, leaving4.0405 µA additional load reserve. Nexperia's125°C row then supports VOL≤**0.11 V over VCC0.8–3.6 V**, leaving340 mV to TMUX VIL0.45 V. Valid high is≥VCC−0.11 V, hence≥3.135130 V, or1.935130 V above TMUX VIH1.2 V. R33=220 kΩ also fits the20 µA row but leaves only0.868 µA reserve. The43 kΩ option instead relies on the discrete3.0 V/2.7 mA VOL0.36 V row and its unresolved extension to the actual supply range. [Nexperia pp7–8](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74AUP1G97.pdf)

At VCC=0, AUP Ioff≤0.75 µA is explicitly specified for input/output0–3.6 V. If an independent pullup remained live,0.75+2 µA through297 kΩ would leave high≥2.428380 V. **Here U14 and R33 share3V3, so this is not a promise of high during rail loss.** Partial supply, including0.2–0.8 V, remains unqualified. A270 kΩ resistor-only recovery would be slower if the driver were actually high-impedance, but it does not set normal push-pull rise time.

Nexperia characterizes propagation delay at3.0–3.6 V/125°C for5/10/15/30 pF loads (max4.3/5.1/5.7/7.4 ns), using input tr/tf≤3 ns and a1 MΩ measurement load. These are **propagation**, not output-rise, limits; they do not establish nanosecond end-to-end timing for the slow input or this pullup load. Actual output capacitance/edges and TMUX enable timing remain required. Keep local bypass and verify slow-input supply current/noise, valid-range threshold interpretation and startup/brownout behavior. [Nexperia pp8–11](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74AUP1G97.pdf)

The Schmitt alternative addresses the specific slow-input concern and can obtain a continuous-supply **output DC** margin with the270 kΩ proposal. It is a conditional engineering candidate, not full-supply/ramp/timing or bench qualification.
